# Characters

## center
This is the character list. Every chat starts from a card here.

## .charSearchWrap
Search filters by name, and by the tags on a card.

## .card.pickerCard
Click a card to open its page. The page holds the description, the greetings and the chat history.

## .card.pickerCard > .pickerAvatarButton
Click the character's icon to jump directly to your most recent chat with them.

## .importButton
Import a character card. PNG cards and JSON files are both supported.

## button[title="New character"]
New characters are created here.
